Documentation & Policies

Everything you need to know about how DK Global Technologies works with clients — our process, what to expect at each stage, and the policies that govern our website and services.

Overview

This page is the central reference for how DK Global Technologies operates — from the first consultation to post-launch support — along with the legal policies that apply whenever you use our website or engage us for a project. If you can't find what you're looking for, reach out through our contact page and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.

Getting Started With a Project

Every engagement — whether it's a website build, an SEO campaign, or a full digital marketing plan — follows the same starting point: a free, no-obligation consultation. We use this call to understand your goals, current challenges and budget before proposing a scope and timeline. There's no commitment required to request a quote.

Our Process

1. Discovery — We audit your current website, brand assets and competitive landscape, and align on the outcomes that matter most to your business.

2. Proposal & Scope — You receive a written proposal outlining deliverables, timeline and pricing before any work begins.

3. Design & Build — For website projects, we share progress at key milestones so you can review and request changes early, not just at the end.

4. Launch — We handle deployment, testing across devices, and a final quality check before your project goes live.

5. Support — Every package includes a free post-launch support window, with ongoing maintenance and growth retainers available afterward.

Working With Us

We're based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, and work with clients across India as well as internationally. Communication happens over email, phone and scheduled calls — you'll always have a single point of contact for your project rather than being passed between departments.

"Clear scope, honest timelines, and no surprise invoices — that's the standard we hold ourselves to on every project, regardless of size."

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sign a contract before starting work?

Yes, every project begins with a written agreement covering scope, timeline, payment terms and deliverables.

What if I need changes after launch?

Minor fixes are covered under your free post-launch support, larger changes are quoted separately or covered under a maintenance retainer.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes — while we specialize in work with clients across India and internationally.
